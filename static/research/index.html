Research portfolio

Heesoo Jang

Assistant Professor of Media Law and Ethics · University of Massachusetts Amherst

My research asks who governs what a democracy knows. I study how dependence on platforms and AI companies redistributes power and accountability across journalism, courts, election systems, and the communities they serve.

I combine qualitative, quantitative, and computational methods across national contexts in service of a more democratic and just society.
